# X thread: M-Pesa payment trends among Nairobi SMEs

*19 November 2026 | 14 sources | 4 web searches | cost $0.0494*

1/ M-Pesa merchant payments are growing fast, and the numbers are worth a closer look. Here is what the latest data shows. 🧵

2/ In the six months to September 2026, M-Pesa merchant transactions surged 52.6% to 4.7 billion. [3][4][5]

3/ Merchant services revenue rose 34.6% to KSh8.18 billion over the same period. [3][4][5]

4/ Safaricom's merchant network reached about 3.13 million businesses in FY2026, a 71% expansion. Counts differ across sources, so don't compare them directly. [2][3]

5/ Lipa na M-PESA pricing changed on 7 August 2026. The free band rose from KES 200 to KES 500, leaving roughly 81% of merchant transactions free. [1][6][9]

6/ Registered mobile-money accounts hit 95.31 million in August 2026. Monthly transaction counts fell from 309.28 million in 2024 to 217.58 million in 2025 as payments shifted toward smaller values. [10][11]

7/ Takeaway: no Nairobi-specific dataset on SME M-Pesa use exists yet. Anchor decisions on Safaricom and CBK figures, not unverified vendor claims. [7][12][14]

## Key points

- M-Pesa merchant transactions surged 52.6% to 4.7 billion in the six months to September 2026, with merchant services revenue up 34.6% to KSh8.18 billion. [3][4][5]
- Safaricom's merchant network reached about 3.13 million businesses in FY2026, a 71% expansion, though merchant counts differ across sources and should not be compared directly. [2][3]
- Lipa na M-PESA pricing changed on 7 August 2026, with the free band raised from KES 200 to KES 500, leaving roughly 81% of merchant transactions free. [1][6][9]
- Registered mobile-money accounts reached 95.31 million in August 2026, while monthly transaction counts fell from 309.28 million in 2024 to 217.58 million in 2025 as payments shifted toward smaller values. [10][11]
- No Nairobi-specific dataset on M-Pesa use by SMEs was found, and vendor claims such as 4.2% fee burdens and KSh180k reconciliation losses remain unverified, so decisions should rely on Safaricom and CBK figures. [7][12][14]
